AEO vs SEO vs GEO: What’s the Difference in 2026?

SEO earns rankings and clicks in search results, while AEO earns mentions and citations inside direct answers.

Three acronyms now describe the work of being found online, and vendors often use them to mean different things.

Strip the labels away and the differences come down to what you track and how you measure success.

Key takeaways

The term GEO comes from a research paper first published in November 2023.
AEO and GEO cover the same platforms: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini.
Pick one term for your team. What you track matters more than the label.

What is SEO?

Definition

Search engine optimization is the practice of improving your pages so they rank higher in organic search results.

Two things are different:

Query fan-out
AI features use query fan-out, running several related searches before answering.
Prompt tracking
AEO and GEO results need prompt tracking, because classic rank trackers don’t record AI answers.
