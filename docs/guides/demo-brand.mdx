title: "Demo brand"
description: "Explore Ansvisor on a sample brand before you pick a plan, or after yours has ended."
---

The **demo brand** is **Brewline Coffee**, a made-up coffee roaster with a month of sample data. It lets you look around the live product, page by page, before Ansvisor works on your own brand. It's an Ansvisor Cloud feature.

## Who sees it

- **New workspaces without a card.** The plan step of sign-up offers **Not ready to add a card? Explore a demo brand**. Your own brand stays saved, and tracking starts on it when you pick a plan.
- **Workspaces whose trial ended or whose plan was cancelled.** They keep their own data, read-only, and the read-only banner offers **See the demo**.
- **Everyone in the workspace.** Once it exists, the demo brand is in the brand menu as **Demo · sample data**. There's one per workspace.

## What it shows

The demo brand is an ordinary brand in your own workspace, so every page reads it the way it reads yours: the same screens, filters, tabs and links. The sample data includes:
- a dozen tracked prompts and their answers from six AI platforms;
- the citations and mentions in those answers;
- four competitors;
- shopping cards, search volumes and query fan-outs.

Everything in it is fictional. Brewline, its competitors and every site it's cited on live on `.example` domains, which can't belong to a real company.

A band across the top says you're on the demo brand, with the way to your own brand. Chat shows as **Soon (next week)**, with a preview. **Settings → Integrations** shows the integrations connected with sample data, and those still to come as coming soon.

## What it can't do

Nothing on the demo brand runs, uses AI or costs anything:
- it's never tracked, audited or synced, and no job or e-mail runs for it;
- it never counts against your plan's brands or prompts.

Anything that would run or use AI, such as tracking, generating, improving, auditing or sending, opens the plans instead. What they offer depends on the workspace:

| Workspace | Offer |
|---|---|
| Never had a plan | **14 days free, then 50% off** |
| Trial ended, or plan cancelled | **50% off your first month** |

The demo brand can't be changed or deleted.

## Hide it, show it again

Admins and managers hide the demo brand from the brand menu with **Hide the demo brand**, and bring it back with **Show the demo brand**. Hiding is for the whole workspace. The demo brand is hidden once you pick a plan, and it's never deleted: it can be shown again, for a colleague who joins or after a plan ends.

## How it stays current

The demo brand follows the product, without anyone updating it:

- It's rebuilt on the next visit when Ansvisor changes what the demo shows, or when its data is more than 3 days old. Its dates always end around today.
- A rebuild keeps the brand and its pages' addresses, so a saved link to the demo still opens.
- Sample data that nobody opened for 14 days is cleared, and built again on the next visit.

## Self-hosting

The demo brand is part of Ansvisor Cloud. A self-hosted workspace has no plan step and never turns read-only, so nothing offers it. Migration `00380_demo_brand.sql` still runs on every install: it adds the demo columns to `brands` and changes nothing for your own brands.
