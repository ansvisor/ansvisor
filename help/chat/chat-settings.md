**Settings** in the chat menu, right under **Inbox**, holds your own Chat preferences. They are yours alone and follow you to every device. A change is kept as soon as you make it; if it can't be kept, it goes back and Chat says so. The bar next to **Settings** shows how much of this month's AI usage your workspace has used.

## Default mode

**Default mode** is how much the agent does on its own when you start a job: **Ask me first**, **Auto, ask before changes** or **Full auto**. New chats start in it. Nothing is published or sent without your click. **Full auto** needs the Growth plan, and your Admin can turn it off for everyone but Admins; when it is locked, point at it to see why. Analysts and Agency partners only read, so they have no default mode.

## Your focus

**Your focus** lists the eight areas Chat can focus on for you, such as **Competitors** or **Content**. Tick the ones you work on: Chat Home and its suggestions follow them. Your focus doesn't change what you can do; your role decides that.

## Answer language and notifications

**Answer language** sets the language Chat answers in: **Same as my question**, English, Türkçe or Deutsch. The app itself stays in English.

**Notify me when an agent needs me** chooses where you hear about it: **Inbox + email** or **Inbox only**. **Inbox + Slack** comes once Slack can be connected.

## Thought for N s and Chat on every page

**Show “Thought for N s”** shows or hides the short summary of the agent's reasoning under answers.

**Open Chat on every page** shows or hides **Ask Chat** with Ctrl J in each page's header.

Workspace rules, such as which tools Chat may use on its own, are in **Tool policy**; only Admins change them.
