**What's new** tells you what Ansvisor added, in a few short lines: why we built it, what it does, how it helps you and what you can do with it. The Ansvisor team writes each update. You see the ones your plan includes.

## On Chat Home

When there is an update you haven't read, a line with a **New** tag shows under the shortcuts, for up to 14 days. Click **Show me** to put "What's new: how can {feature} help me?" in the box. Nothing is sent until you send it.

The **What's new** shortcut asks Chat what's new. A small dot on it means you have updates you haven't read.

## The answer

Chat shows up to three updates as cards: the one you asked about first, then the ones you haven't read, then the newest. Each card has **Try it**, which opens the feature's page for the brand open now. In the side panel, it opens the page behind the panel.

An update your plan doesn't include says which plans do, with **See plans**. Ask for **Show older updates** to see earlier ones.

Reading What's new reads none of your brand's data and uses no AI usage. If you write in another language, Chat gives the updates in it; that answer uses some AI usage.

## When an update counts as read

An update counts as read once Chat shows it in an answer, or once you open its **Try it**. What you've read is yours alone, the same in the side panel and on the full page.

## Get it every month

Ask Chat "Send me What's new every month" to make a scheduled job, **What's new for my plan**. It runs on the 1st of every month at 9:00 unless you pick another time on its card. You can set where results go, but there is no prompt to write.

Each run lists the updates for your plan that you haven't read, up to ten, in the job's own chat, and marks them read. With nothing new, it sends nothing and its run shows as unchanged. It uses no AI usage. Find, pause or delete it on the **Scheduled** page.
