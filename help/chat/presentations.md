Chat can turn a saved report into an editable PowerPoint deck. Ask in your own words, for example "Turn the October report into a presentation". Admins and Managers can make presentations.

The deck keeps the report's own numbers: its summary, key figures, visibility trend, share of voice, competitors, best and worst prompts, topics, and top citation sources, each on its own slides. Nothing is recalculated. Every slide with figures names the report and its period at the bottom, and a section the report could not gather is listed on a closing slide instead of being left out. When the report holds more rows than a slide shows, the slide says how many it shows.

## Approve the deck

Before the deck is made, Chat shows a card that says it will make a presentation. Click **Approve** to make it. In Full auto the deck is made without the card.

## Open and download

The deck is kept in **Files** in Chat, counting toward your workspace's storage like any other file there. Open it from the run's card or from Files, and download it as a .pptx you can edit in PowerPoint, Keynote or LibreOffice.
