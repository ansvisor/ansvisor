The product tour shows you Ansvisor's main pages one at a time. Each topic opens its page with Chat in the side panel beside it, where a few short lines explain what the page shows. The tour's text is written ahead, so it uses none of your AI usage. In the free trial, and while you explore the demo brand, Chat Home offers it on its welcome card.

## Start the tour

On Chat Home's welcome card, **Want a quick tour?** lists the topics. Pick one to start there, or click **Show me everything** to start with the first. **Not now** hides the card for good; you can still take the tour later from Chat's settings.

## The topics

The tour has five topics, in this order:

- **Visibility**: the Visibility page and its Overview, Mentions and Citations tabs.
- **Topics and prompts**: the Prompts page, with Tracked Prompts, Topics and Prompt Suggestions.
- **Positions and locations**: Tracked Prompts' Positions column and its Locations view.
- **Content**: Content's Opportunities, Plan and Library.
- **Connections**: Settings › Integrations, for Google Search Console, Google Analytics and your CMS.

## Move through the tour

The panel says where you are, such as **Ansvisor tour · 2 of 5**. Under each explanation:

- **Next** opens the next topic's page, with the panel still open.
- On Prompts, **Suggest prompts for me** asks Chat for prompt ideas, as any question does.
- **Another topic** lists the other topics to jump to.
- **End the tour** ends it with one last line on where to find Chat from now on.

## The panel on every page

While the tour is on, every page you open, from the menu too, opens with Chat beside it on the tour. On a page the tour explained, the panel brings that page's explanation into view and marks it for a moment, with the tour's chips under it. On a topic's page it hasn't explained yet, **Show me this page** explains it. On other pages the panel stays on the latest explanation.

If you close the panel, it stays closed on the next pages until you open Chat again or click one of the tour's chips.

## While your first results come in

If your brand's first tracking run is still going, each explanation ends with a line saying your first results are still coming in, with about how long is left when known. Visibility, Prompts and Content say the same where they have nothing to show yet.

## Ask your own questions

You can type a question in the panel at any point of the tour. It goes to Chat like any other question, in the same chat, and uses AI as usual; on the demo brand, asking opens the plans. The tour's chips come back under the answer, so you can go on.

## The tour in your language

The tour follows **Answer language** in Chat's settings. With a language picked there, its lines come in that language; the names of pages, tabs and buttons stay as the app shows them, in English. With **Same as my question**, the tour is in English.

## Take the tour again

The tour is kept as a chat named **Ansvisor tour** in your chats, so you can read it again. To take it again, open **Settings** in the chat menu and click **Take the tour** under **Product tour**. It starts from the first topic and adds to the same chat.

## When the tour isn't offered

The tour runs in Chat's side panel. If you turned off **Open Chat on every page** in Chat's settings, the welcome card's topics only open their pages, and **Take the tour** doesn't show.
