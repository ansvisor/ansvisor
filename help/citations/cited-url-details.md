Every URL that AI engines cite has its own page in Ansvisor. Open it from the **URLs** tab of the Citations page by clicking a URL. Its header shows the page's title, its address and its source type.

## Answers that cited it

**Cited in** lists every AI answer that cited the URL in the selected window, with the date, the AI platform and the tracked prompt that triggered the citation. Use it to see exactly which questions lead AI engines to this page.

## Turn it into outreach

A review or list article that AI engines cite but that doesn't name your brand is a direct outreach target: ask its publisher to include you. If the URL is a competitor's page, it is an opportunity for a counter-piece of your own that answers the same question.
