The **Citations** page gathers the URLs that AI engines cited across your tracked prompts, with its domain and the number of times it was cited. It shows which sources AI engines rely on when they answer questions in your category, and sorts them by source type.

## Domains and URLs

Under **Top Sources**, the **Domains** tab lists the cited sources by domain and the **URLs** tab by single page. Four buttons above them choose the sources listed: **Brand** (your own domains, the default), **Competitors**, **Third-Party** or **All**. Click a URL to open its own page, which shows the AI answers that cited it and the prompt behind each. **Source Types** shows how the cited sources split by type.

## Source types

Ansvisor classifies every cited source automatically. Your own pages and your competitors' pages are marked as such, and other sources get a type such as **Review**, **Forum** or **Social**. The types matter differently:

- Your own pages are the easiest to optimize.
- Review sites are weighted heavily by AI engines for buying-intent prompts.
- Forums are surprisingly influential for technical and B2B questions.
- Social sites carry the lowest weight in current AI models.
- When AI cites competitors' pages, you are losing ground in comparisons.

## Find competitor pages AI cites

Above the Domains and URLs lists, choose **Competitors** to see the competitor pages AI engines cite when they answer questions in your category. Each one is an opportunity for a counter-piece: content of your own that answers the same question.
