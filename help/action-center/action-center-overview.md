The **Action Center** is where Ansvisor turns what changed in AI search into a ranked list of work. It is the first page you see after you sign in. Everything in it is per brand: switch brands to see another brand's work.

## The tabs

- **Actions**: what to do now, ranked by Priority Score.
- **Signals**: what changed across your AI visibility data.
- **KPIs**: the goals you track for the brand.
- **History**: what was done, and what it moved.

Admins and managers also see **Needs review**, where tasks the AI wrote without some of their details wait for a person.

## How the work flows

Signals are detected after your daily tracking run. Ansvisor groups related signals into one action, so a long list of findings becomes a few pieces of work. At most five new actions are added per brand per day, so the list stays short enough to act on. Each action comes with tasks. When you close an action as **Completed**, History measures what changed afterwards.

## While the first analysis runs

While a new brand's first analysis runs, a **First analysis running** note sits under the tabs. Actions, signals and KPIs fill in once it finishes. Meanwhile, a new brand starts with the **Getting started** steps on the Actions tab.

## A weekly routine

Work from the **Top Actions** card each week. Assign each action, set a due date, and close it as **Completed** when its tasks are done, so History can measure the result.
