**Today** is the first tab of the **Action Center**: what changed since your last visit, what to do today and what is waiting for you, on one page per brand. Open it from **Action Center**, first under **Execution** in the main menu. When you sign in, Ansvisor opens on **Prompts › Tracked Prompts**, whatever the plan; Today is one click away. When Prompts is off your menu, Ansvisor opens here instead.

## The tabs

The Action Center has five tabs: **Today**, **Actions**, **Signals**, **Agents** and **History**. **Agents** (formerly Agent Center) is there when Chat's agents are on for your workspace and on your menu. **KPIs** opens from **History**, next to its Past actions and Results by kind views. Admins and managers open **Needs review** from **Waiting for you** on Today.

Beside **Agents** is how many of your agent runs wait for you.

## The top of the page

The top line has the date, the brand and when AI answers last came in, with how many changes and things wait for you. Under the tabs, the loop strip follows your brand's daily loop: **Prompts** (how many are tracked: active prompts only, not paused ones or those waiting for a spot), **AI answers**, **Actions** (open ones: new, in progress or on hold, whoever has them, the same number as **All** on the Actions tab; under it, how many are on hold and how many are yours), **Done** (this week) and **Results** (your score out of 100 and its change).

## Waiting for you and Where you stand

Under the loop strip sit two cards (stacked on a narrow screen):

- **Waiting for you**: this brand's agent work waiting for your OK, drafts waiting for your review (whoever wrote them, linked to **Library › In review**), tasks due today or overdue, weekly reminders, and for admins and managers, tasks in **Needs review** and new prompt ideas from Search Console or Analytics. Sections off your menu are left out.
- **Where you stand**: your Visibility score out of 100 from the latest run, dated, its change since the run before, your rank among primary rivals (marked **Direct competitor**) or, with none marked, competitors named in 10+ prompts, and the gap to the next brand. Before a score is in, it says so.

Each card keeps its place and fills in once its own data is in. To ask about any of it, use **Ask Chat** in the header, on every page.

## Since yesterday and Today's focus

Both run across the page, under the two cards.

- **Since yesterday**: up to 5 rows of the biggest changes since your last visit, marked **Up**, **Down** or **Rival**; click a row to open its action. Changes of one kind share a row ("5 rivals are mentioned far more than you · 1 action"); **Show all** lists each. Low-impact ones are in **All signals**. On a Monday it reads "Since Friday". A quiet day says so.
- **Today's focus**: your three highest-impact actions, picked as Chat's daily suggestion picks them and shown highest impact first (Critical, High, Medium, Low), then by priority. Each card's first step names the action's own prompts, pages or rivals, the same count as its title. They stay until you finish them; **Skip for now** hides one from your focus for 7 days without changing it. A new one is marked **New**.

## Finish setting up

Under **Today's focus**, **Finish setting up** lists the next of the 7 set-up steps (the AI traffic snippet, KPIs, competitors, topics, prompts, Search Console, Analytics), until all 7 are done. **Hide** hides the steps for the brand when an admin or manager hides them, and for you otherwise.

## Prompts waiting for a spot

When your plan's prompt locations are all in use, prompts you add are saved as **Waiting for a spot**: kept, but not tracked. Admins and managers see them under **Waiting for you**, with **Free up a spot**, and on Starter, **See Growth**. A waiting prompt never starts by itself when a spot frees up; Today says "1 spot free" instead, so you pick which one to track.

**Free up a spot** opens Tracked Prompts and lists prompts you could pause, least useful first: low demand, and AI rarely names you there. Your other brands' prompts are listed too, since the limit is shared. A prompt in several locations needs that many spots. **Pause it and track this one** does both at once; if tracking fails, the paused prompts are tracked again.

## A brand's first day

Before the first answers are in, Today asks you to check the prompts we track for you: remove any that don't fit, add your own, then **Looks good**. Suggestions already stored for the brand show under **Worth tracking too**, and **While you wait** lists the set-up steps left and Chat's tour.

## Mondays

On Mondays, once the brand has a full week of answers, **Last week** sums up visibility against the week before, the actions done and the results that came in, with a link to Reports. The focus reads **This week's focus**.

## New area to track

On your Monday, or once today's focus is finished, admins and managers may see **New area to track** under the focus: one topic you don't track yet, when what Ansvisor already has makes a strong case for it. That is a catalog category with 3 or more products, 3 or more Search Console or Analytics prompt ideas about it, or prompt ideas from ChatGPT Business Hub. The card says why, and how many prompt spots it needs and has left on your plan. It waits until your workspace is two weeks old.

**Suggest 5 prompts** adds the topic and lists the prompt ideas you already have for it; with none, it writes 5. Tick the ones to track: past your plan's spots, they are saved as waiting for a spot. **Not now** hides that topic for you for 30 days.

## After a plan or trial ends

Today is read-only: what your trial found, the focus waiting for a plan, what is saved for you, and **Choose a plan**.
