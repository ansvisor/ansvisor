ChatGPT Business Hub is OpenAI's free place for a business to claim its profile in ChatGPT and correct what ChatGPT says about it. The **Set up your ChatGPT Business Hub profile** action walks you through it, one task at a time, in the Action Center.

## What Business Hub is

In Business Hub you claim your business, verify it is yours, and correct the facts ChatGPT shows: name, category, address or service area, hours, contact details, products and services. An online shop can also connect its product feed. Claiming the profile doesn't move you up in answers by itself, and OpenAI may still fill in a field from other sources, so keep the same facts on your site. Open Business Hub from ChatGPT, or with **Open Business Hub** in the action. **Settings › Integrations** lists Business Hub as a manual setup, with nothing to connect since it has no API yet, and its **Set up your profile** opens this action.

## When the action appears

Ansvisor raises it once for each brand, on a nightly run, when the brand has a website and ChatGPT is tracked: at least one ChatGPT answer in the last 30 days. Its **Why** says how many of those answers named you and cited your site. Once it is completed or dismissed, it doesn't come back.

## The steps

1. **Claim** your business.
2. **Verify** it is yours. Your website domain is the surest way; Business Hub may also ask for an ID check.
3. **Correct your business facts**, using the facts as your own site states them. **Check what ChatGPT says** in the action opens Chat with the **ChatGPT profile check**: it lists what ChatGPT gets wrong and writes the corrections to paste, asking you for anything nothing confirms.
4. **Connect your product feed**, for an online shop only. If you already send OpenAI a product feed, this task is done from the start.
5. **Turn on your Business Agent**, in the US only, and only if Business Hub offers it.
6. **Enter your first Business Hub numbers** on Visibility's ChatGPT Business Hub card. **Enter numbers** in the action opens it.

Each task says why it matters, how to do it and when it is done.

## Outside the US

Business Hub may not be open in every country yet. Outside the countries where claiming is open, the action is called **Get your business into ChatGPT Business Hub** and has low impact. Its first task says to join the waitlist if Business Hub offers one, and to mark the task done. There is no Business Agent task.

## Why you mark the steps yourself

Business Hub has no connection Ansvisor can read, so Ansvisor can't see your progress there. Mark each task done when its **Done when** is true, for example when Business Hub shows your business as verified. Only the last one completes itself, when your first numbers are saved. The action closes the way any action does. Ansvisor doesn't measure a result for it.

## Who can do it

Every member of the workspace can work the tasks and mark them done, as with any action. Claiming and verifying need someone who can sign in to your ChatGPT account and, for the domain check, add a record at your domain host.
