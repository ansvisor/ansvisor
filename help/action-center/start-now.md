When an action's work is writing, a new page or a better existing one, its drawer opens with a **Start now** block at the top of **Overview**. It takes you straight to the writing, with what the action knows already filled in.

## What to do for your kind of site

When an action asks for new content for prompts or a topic, Ansvisor reads what those prompts ask and picks the page to write. Overview then starts with **What to do for an online store** (or a SaaS, an app, a travel brand) and the page, such as **Write a comparison page**, with why: how many of the prompts ask for it and their share of the estimated AI searches. A page qualifies when at least 15% of the searches ask for it.

**Also worth doing here** lists up to two more pages the same prompts ask for. When the prompts' search intents aren't known yet, nothing is picked.

**Start now** sits under it and writes that kind of page.

## What Content Editor opens

Click **Content Editor**. A draft is written for the action's writing task and opens in the Content Editor:

- the action's prompts, so its sources and brief answer them;
- the page it works on, read from your site, for an existing page;
- the page type, from the page's address, or from the kind of page the action picked;
- on an online shop, the products the action is about, locked into the page's shopping path.

A new draft builds its brief as it opens, from your AI usage. The task moves to **In Progress**. Once a draft is written, the button reads **Open draft** and opens it as you left it.

## Several pages

When an action works on several pages, **Content Editor** writes one draft for each of them and opens the **Library** with only that action's drafts. Click **Show all drafts** to see the rest again. Each draft builds its brief when you ask for it in the editor.

## Chat

**Chat** works the action's tasks with you in a chat, in your default mode, beside the action. Closing tasks stays your click. Until Chat is on for your workspace, the button shows **Soon** and does nothing. Admins and Managers start it.

## From the Actions list

On an action's row, its menu offers **Start in Content Editor**, which does what the drawer's button does without opening the action.

## What stays yours

Nothing is written or started on its own: each draft and each chat starts with your click, and nothing is published. On the demo brand, or when your plan has ended, both buttons open the plan dialog instead.
