In the Content Editor you can generate an image for the page with AI, such as a cover. It goes into the page like any other image and is marked **AI-generated**.

## Open Generate with AI

Click the image button in the editor's toolbar, then **Generate with AI**. You can also click an empty line and pick **Generate image**. The other tab, **Upload or address**, adds an image of your own.

## Describe the image

The description starts from the page's title; change it to say what the image should show. Pick a style (**Brand style**, **Illustration** or **Photo-like**) and a shape (16:9 for a cover, 1:1 or 4:3).

Images never show a product's screens, realistic people, or charts with numbers. They have no text unless your description asks for it.

## Pick a model and how many

The first model is marked **Recommended**: it uses your AI limit most slowly. Each model shows how fast it uses your monthly AI limit compared with that one (**1x**, **2x**, **4x**) and about how much one image uses. Choose 2, 3 or 4 options. Under the generate button you see about how much of your monthly AI limit they use together. On your workspace's own OpenRouter key, models say **Your key** and don't use your limit.

## Pick one and add it

Click **Generate 2 images** (or 3 or 4). Click the option you like, check the **Alt text** (it says what the image shows, for people who can't see it and for search), and click **Use this image**. Only that one is kept and added to the page; the other options are removed, also when you close the dialog (any still left are removed after a day). **Regenerate** makes new options and uses your AI limit again.

If what's left of your monthly AI limit doesn't cover the options you chose, none is made: choose fewer. If an image is made but can't be saved, the panel says so: it still counts toward your AI limit, and you pick from the others. The demo brand makes no images.

## Where AI images count

In the editor, an AI image shows **AI-generated** under it. The page keeps a note that AI made it, and its alt text, in **Copy HTML**, the HTML export and what goes to a CMS that takes images. Page quality never counts it as the page's own visual proof, and you can't confirm it as your own image. Add a photo or screenshot you took for that.
