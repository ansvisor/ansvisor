**Ads** is an internal preview. It's in development, and nobody sees it in production until it launches. Where it's turned on, it sits under **Analytics**, after **AI Referral Traffic**, with a **New** pill. Ads will show, for each buying-intent prompt, whether to earn the AI answer with content, pay for an ad, or do both. Today the page has its tabs and no data yet.

## What the page shows today

Five tabs: **Overview**, **Opportunities**, **Paid SoV**, **Campaigns** and **Connections**. Point at **Paid SoV** to see what it stands for: your share of the sponsored ads shown in AI answers. Each tab says what it will show, and none shows numbers yet. The period switch (**7d**, **30d**, **90d**) and **Export** beside the tabs stay off until there's data.

## Plans

Ads is included from the Growth plan. On Starter and in the free trial, each tab shows what Growth adds, with **Upgrade**.

## On the menu

Ads is a menu section like the others. Admins, and the managers they let, can take it off a role's menu or a person's in **Settings › Menu access**. People who pick what they work on see it with **Founders & leaders**, **Growth** and **Analytics**.
