The **Campaigns** tab of **Ads** will list your campaigns once an ad account is connected. No ad account can be connected yet, so its table is empty and says **Connect an ad account · coming soon**. Nothing reaches an ad platform. The columns show what will come: **Campaign**, **Channel**, **Status**, **Spend**, **AI-assisted signups** and **Cost per signup**.

## Your plan

The **Your plan** strip names your plan:

- On Growth, Campaigns will be read-only: you'll see spend and results once an ad account is connected. Building and changing campaigns needs Enterprise or the Ads add-on. **Compare plans** shows the plans. **Add Ads add-on** opens our contact form: talk to us, and for now we set up the add-on with you.
- On Enterprise, and on a self-hosted install, you'll build and change campaigns once an ad account is connected. Every enable, budget or bid change still needs your click.

## Safety

The **Safety** card sets out rules that Ansvisor enforces, not an agent:

- Every enable, pause, budget or bid change needs a person's click and confirmation, in every mode.
- Agents never spend: an agent can suggest a change, and only a person can make it.
- Ad spend is paid to Google, Microsoft and OpenAI from your own ad accounts. Ansvisor never sees or changes payment methods.

Today, nothing reaches an ad account.

## On Starter and in the free trial

The tab shows what the Growth plan adds, with **Upgrade**.
