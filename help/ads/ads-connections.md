The **Connections** tab of **Ads** lists the ad platforms Ads will work with, and Google Analytics. No ad account can be connected yet, so nothing reaches an ad platform from Ansvisor.

## ChatGPT Ads, Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising

These three show **Coming soon**. **Google Ads** includes ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode, and **Microsoft Advertising** includes ads in Copilot.

Billing stays with each platform: ChatGPT Ads Manager, Google Ads or Microsoft Advertising. Ansvisor never sees or changes how you pay for ads.

## Meta Ads and LinkedIn Ads

These show **Coming later**. Click **Notify me** to hear when that platform is ready; the card then says **We'll tell you**. On the demo brand, **Notify me** opens the plans.

## Google Analytics

The **Google Analytics** card shows your brand's connection: **Connected**, **Not connected**, **Sign-in failed** or **Couldn't check**. **Connect** or **Manage** opens **Settings › Integrations**. It's the same connection Ansvisor uses everywhere: connected there means connected here.

## On Starter and in the free trial

The tab shows what the Growth plan adds, with **Upgrade**.
