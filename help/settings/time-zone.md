Each person has their own time zone. Find it in **Settings › Account › Time zone**. It starts on **Automatic**, which uses your browser's time zone and shows it beside the word, such as **Automatic (Europe/Istanbul)**.

## Pick a fixed time zone

Click the field and type a city, a region or an offset, such as "new york" or "gmt+3", then pick one from the list. Your choice is saved as soon as you pick it. To go back to your browser's time zone, pick **Automatic**.

A fixed time zone helps when you travel, or when your browser's time zone isn't the one you work in.

## What uses it

- **New scheduled jobs**: the schedule card's **When** line shows your time zone, and the job is made on it.
- **New agent schedules**: a **Schedule** trigger you add to an agent in **Agent Center** starts on your time zone, shown beside its time.
- **Chat's dates and times**: when an answer was made and how old its data is, from your next message on.
- **Daily suggestions**: they arrive in your morning, in this time zone.

## Jobs you already have

Changing your time zone never moves a scheduled job or an agent's Schedule trigger. Each keeps the time zone it was made with. To change a job's time zone, open it from **Scheduled** and pick another under **Time zone**, then click **Save**.
