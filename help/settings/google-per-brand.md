Each brand reads Search Console and Analytics from one Google account. Every brand starts with the workspace's account, the one connected in **Settings › Integrations**. A brand can instead use the same account as another brand, or a Google account of its own, for example a client's.

## Change a brand's Google account

Open the brand's **Settings › Integrations**. Each Google card says which account the brand uses and which other brands use it too. Admins and managers who can see the brand can:

- **Use the same account as…**: pick another brand's account, or the workspace's, without signing in again.
- **Connect another Google account**: sign in with Google in the window that opens. The brand keeps its account until you finish.
- **Disconnect**: the brand stops reading from Google. The workspace's account stays for the other brands.

Then pick the brand's property from the new account's list.

## See which brand uses which account

In **Settings › Integrations**, **Google per brand** lists each brand with its Search Console and Analytics account. Click **Change** to open that brand's settings.
