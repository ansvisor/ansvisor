Everyone starts on **Everything**: every section of the left menu their role shows. Each person can then say what they work on or lead, and their left menu keeps only those areas' sections. Pick one or more; picking none keeps every section. **Settings** is always on the menu, with your API keys and MCP.

## The work areas

- **Founders & leaders**: Action Center, Agents, Chat, Visibility, Mentions, AI Referral Traffic, Reports, Competitors.
- **AEO/GEO & Audit**: Action Center, Agents, Content, Audit, Citations, AI Referral Traffic, Reports.
- **Content**: Action Center, Agents, Chat, Content, Prompts, Citations, Reports.
- **Brand & PR**: Action Center, Agents, Chat, Visibility, Mentions, Citations, Reports, Competitors.
- **Growth**: Action Center, Agents, Chat, Visibility, Prompts, Citations, AI Referral Traffic, Reports, Competitors.
- **Product Development**: Action Center, Agents, Chat, Visibility, Prompts, Mentions, Product Visibility, Reports.
- **Business Development**: Action Center, Agents, Chat, Visibility, Prompts, Mentions, Citations, Reports, Competitors.
- **Developers**: Action Center, Agents, Audit, Prompts, Citations, AI Referral Traffic, Reports.
- **Analytics**: Action Center, Agents, Visibility, Prompts, Mentions, Citations, Product Visibility, AI Referral Traffic, Reports, Competitors.

## Where you pick them

Nobody is asked at sign-up or on joining: new people, invited teammates and the demo start on **Everything**. Work areas picked before November 11, 2026, such as at sign-up, went back to **Everything** then; pick them again to narrow your menu.

For your first 14 days in the workspace, Chat Home shows **Agents for you**: ready-to-use agents for your brand's site type and your work areas. It shows on Home's Default view too.

## Change your work areas

Pick or change them any time in **Settings › Account › Your work areas**; **Everything** shows every section. It also shows your menu, and why a section is off it: **Hidden by your admin** or **Not in your work areas**.

When something is off your menu, the bottom of the sidebar says how much, such as **3 sections off your menu**, with **Change** leading to your work areas.
