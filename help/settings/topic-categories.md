On an online store, each topic you track can be about one or more categories of your product catalog: the topic "cold brew" and the category "Coffee > Cold Brew". The link lets a topic's work reach the category's page and its products.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings › Integrations** and go to **Product catalog**. **Topics and categories** is under **Category pages**, once your brand has topics and its catalog has categories.

## How they're linked

Ansvisor matches them for you: a category is a topic's when every word of its own name (the last part, "Cold Brew") is in the topic's name, or in at least two of the topic's prompts. Up to three categories are matched per topic, the closest first. Each shows **matched**.

## Change a link

Admins and managers can change them; everyone else sees the list.

- **Take a category away:** select **×** on it. It stays away, even when the words still match.
- **Add a category:** pick one under **Add a category**. It shows **set by hand**.

Matching runs again whenever the list is read, so a new topic or category is linked without a sync.
