Competitors are the brands Ansvisor compares with yours in AI answers to your tracked prompts. You add them on the **Competitors** page, and each one then appears in **Tracked Competitors** for a head-to-head comparison.

## Add a competitor

Click **Add Competitor**. Enter the **Name** exactly as it appears in AI answers: the brand name, not its web address. The **Domain** is optional and is used to cross-check citations. Click **Add**. When a competitor is added, Ansvisor reads back 90 days of your answers once, to find the first time AI cited one of its pages.

## How many to track

Aim for three to eight competitors. With fewer than three, the benchmarks have little signal; with more than eight, the comparison view gets noisy.

## Find competitors you missed

Even if you don't add them, AI answers to your prompts name other brands. Review those names once a month: a brand you've never heard of that is cited three or more times across your prompts is a competitor AI engines already know about. Add it to your list.
