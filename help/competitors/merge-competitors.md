When one company is tracked as two competitors, say under two names or two websites, you can merge them into one on the **Competitors** page. Only admins and managers merge and unmerge competitors.

## Merge competitors

Click **Merge** in **Tracked Competitors** (point at it: "Merge duplicate competitors", such as two domains of one brand). Tick the competitors that are the same company, choose **Keep this one** on the one to keep, read what will happen and click **Merge**.

The one you keep keeps its name, type and logo. The names and domains of the others become its other names, shown as **Also:** on its card. From the merge on, an answer that names any of them, or cites their sites, counts for the one you kept.

The others leave your list, comparisons, share of voice, Chat and the Action Center. A merged competitor can't be added again: unmerge it instead.

When two competitors share a website or a name, the page asks **These may be the same company**. Click **Merge…** to start with both, or close it.

## Numbers from before the merge

Numbers from before the merge aren't combined. Each merged competitor keeps its own history, hidden while it is merged, and the one you kept shows only its own numbers for those days. Answers count together from the merge on.

## Unmerge a competitor

Open **Merged competitors** under the list. It shows what was merged into what, when and by whom. Click **Unmerge** on one, then **Unmerge** again to confirm.

It comes back as its own competitor, with its numbers from before the merge, and its names stop counting for the one you kept. Answers counted for the kept competitor while they were merged stay with it.

Removing a competitor also removes the ones merged into it.

## When the workspace is read-only

On Ansvisor Cloud, while the workspace is read-only, **Merge** and **Unmerge** open the plan dialog instead.
