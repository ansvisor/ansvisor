The **Competitors** page, under **Analytics** in the sidebar, compares the AI visibility of the competitors you track with yours, across your tracked prompts and AI platforms. **Tracked Competitors** lists them with their visibility; select one to see it head-to-head with your brand.

## Head-to-head comparison

The comparison shows **Your Avg. Score**, **Their Avg. Score** and the **Score Difference**. **Gaps** lists the prompts where the competitor leads you, and **Strengths** the prompts where you lead. In **Prompt-by-Prompt Comparison**, click a prompt to expand it and see your visibility against the competitor on that single prompt, platform by platform.

## Platform Breakdown

Some competitors dominate one AI platform but underperform on another. **Platform Breakdown** shows these differences, so you can aim your work at the platforms where a competitor is ahead.

## The competitor's pages over time

Below the comparison, a card shows the competitor's pages over time: why it may be rising. Its chart shows the answers that mentioned the competitor, and your brand, each week for the last twelve weeks, with a marker on each week something happened to its pages. The events are listed newest first:

- **First cited in your tracked answers**: the first day an AI answer to one of your prompts cited one of its pages.
- **Changed**: once a week, Ansvisor reads again the 10 competitor pages your answers cited most over the last two weeks. When a page's words changed by more than a date or a price, it says so, with the Site Audit checks the page gained or lost, such as an FAQ or a table.

## How the pages are read

Pages are read for free from their public address, within a few reads a day, never from a site whose robots.txt keeps crawlers out. The brand's daily page reads are shared with the Action Center's tasks, which go first. Only what Site Audit's checks found and a summary of the page's words are kept, never its text. Nothing is compared with another brand's answers.

## Close a visibility gap

1. Find the prompts where the competitor leads most, under **Gaps**.
2. Inspect the AI answer: which content or citation is the competitor winning with?
3. Create content that targets that prompt.
4. Publish it, earn placements, and watch the gap for two to four weeks to see it close.
