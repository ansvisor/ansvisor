Ansvisor has a light and a dark mode. You can switch between them at any time, from any page.

## Which mode you start in

On Ansvisor Cloud, a new account starts in dark mode for now, from the sign-up page on. Anyone who used Ansvisor before keeps the mode they had. Self-hosted installs start in light mode.

## Switch the mode

Open the main menu: its sun or moon button sits beside the Ansvisor logo, at the top. When the menu is folded to icons, the button is hidden until you open it. On a phone, it is at the top of the menu. The moon switches to dark mode, and the sun to light mode.

Your choice is kept in this browser, so each page opens in it. On another browser or device, Ansvisor opens in its usual mode until you switch there too.

## The account menu

Your name at the bottom of the main menu opens the account menu. At its top are your e-mail, your workspace and its plan, such as **Brewline · Growth**. Below are shortcuts into **Settings**: **Your account**, **Members & invites**, **Integrations**, **API & MCP** and, on Ansvisor Cloud, **Plans & billing**. Then come **What's new** (with Chat on), **Help**, **Send feedback** and **Sign out**. The gear beside your name opens **Settings** as before, and so does ⌘, on a Mac or Ctrl+, elsewhere. When the menu is folded to icons, your avatar opens the same menu. Enter or Space opens it, the arrow keys move through it and Esc closes it. Admins and managers see dots on its items: see **Dots in the menu**.
