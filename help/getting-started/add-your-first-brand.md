When you start with Ansvisor, a setup wizard walks you through your first brand. It takes 5 to 10 minutes, and you need the website of the brand you want to track. The wizard has five steps on a self-hosted install and seven on the cloud. Each step saves as you go, so you can leave and pick up where you stopped.

## Brand basics

- **Brand name**: exactly how the brand is named in the wild, not an abbreviation. Suggestions copy your spelling; mention detection ignores upper and lower case.
- **Website**: your main domain, such as example.com. It's used to classify citations, attribute AI traffic and research competitors.
- **Describe your brand** (optional, but recommended): one to three sentences on what you do and who your audience is. AI uses it to suggest better topics, prompts and competitors.

There's no separate industry field: Ansvisor infers it from your description.

## Market and language

On **Select your target market**, pick the primary **Region** and **Language** for tracking. They decide which language AI engines answer in, the region weighting on engines that localize their answers, such as Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and the search data used to estimate prompt volumes. You can change these later for each prompt if you track several regions.

## Topics

Ansvisor uses your description and an AI model to suggest topics: themes your customers care about. Check the topics that fit your brand and uncheck any that don't apply. You can also type a topic of your own and add it.

Aim for four to seven topics. Too few gives a narrow view; too many is noisy and hard to read.

## Prompts

For each topic you chose, Ansvisor writes starter prompts: real questions a potential customer would ask an AI engine. Review the list: edit a prompt's text inline if the phrasing feels off, delete prompts that don't fit, and add your own at the bottom. The prompts run on every AI platform your plan includes.

Keep prompts free of your brand name. A question about your brand by name almost always scores high, because the AI talks about you when asked, so it doesn't measure how AI engines find you on their own.

## Competitors

Ansvisor suggests competitors for your brand. Check the real rivals, uncheck any that aren't comparable, and add your own at the top, with the name AI answers use and, if you like, its domain. Every plan includes competitor tracking.

## The last steps on the cloud

**What do you work on?** asks what you work on or lead, and your left menu keeps only the sections you need. **Skip: show me everything** keeps every section, and you can change it later in **Settings › Account**. A self-hosted install asks the same the first time you open the dashboard.

The final step picks your plan and starts checkout. Paid plans include a free trial: you enter card details, but you aren't charged until the trial ends. A self-hosted install skips this step.
