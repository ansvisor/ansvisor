When you finish setting up a brand, the first round of questions to the AI engines starts on its own. How long it takes depends on how many prompts you track, and the dashboard updates as the AI platforms report back.

## Where results appear first

- **Brand Visibility**: the first metric cards appear soon after the run starts.
- **Citations**: fills in as AI answers come back with the pages they cite.

## My visibility score is 0

Probably nothing is broken. New, niche or B2B brands often start at 0 on most prompts. AI engines need either plenty of training data that mentions your brand, or live citations from trusted sources. The score stays at 0 only while both are missing, and that is exactly what working on your AI visibility changes.

## Prompts that include my brand name

Remove them. A question that asks about your brand by name almost always scores high, because the AI obviously talks about you when asked. Such prompts don't measure how AI engines discover you on their own, and they distort your averages.

## Competitors I've never heard of

Unknown brands in AI answers are useful: they're the alternatives people see when you aren't cited. Add the most-mentioned ones to your competitors to track them properly.

## Add more topics and prompts later

You can add topics and prompts at any time, from the **Topics** tab of the **Prompts** page or from **Prompt Suggestions**. Tracking picks them up on the next scheduled run.
