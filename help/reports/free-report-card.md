The free AI visibility report card is a one-time snapshot you can ask for on the Ansvisor website with your work e-mail. Ansvisor writes 25 questions your buyers might ask from your home page, asks each one once in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode in your target country and its language, and e-mails you a three-page PDF. One card is made per company every 30 days: a colleague who asks within that time is sent the same card, and each address gets it once.

## What the card measured

- **AI visibility**: the share of the answers that name your brand or your site.
- The same share for each engine.
- The competitors the answers named most, and the sites they cite, with yours marked.
- Three things to do now, and an action plan of four to six steps with the Ansvisor features that work on each. Both are picked from your results by fixed rules.

The questions and answers stay in your target country's language; the PDF is in English.

## Why the app shows other numbers

The card asks each question once, on one day. In Ansvisor, your tracked prompts are asked every day, so the figures move from day to day. The **AI Visibility Score** on **Brand Visibility** is a score from 0 to 100 that also weighs citations and how early you're named, not only the share of answers that name you.

## Track the card's questions every day

After you start a trial, add your brand in the setup wizard, then add the card's questions as prompts in **Tracked Prompts**, in the same country. **Brand Visibility**, **Competitors** and **Citations** then show the same kind of figures for every day. The **Action Center** ranks what to do next.

## The value table and the revenue scenario

The third page prices AI visits at the median Google Ads cost per click of your questions' keywords in your country, against the Starter and Growth plans. A revenue line appears only if you gave an average order or deal value, and its conversion rate is an assumption. Both are scenarios, not a forecast or a guarantee. **AI Referral Traffic** measures your real visits from AI answers once it's set up.
