The free AI visibility report card is a one-time snapshot you can ask for on the Ansvisor website with your work e-mail. Ansvisor finds your main products or categories on your home page, writes the questions your buyers ask about them, keeps the 25 that match what you sell best and that people search for most, asks each one once in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode in your target country and its language, and e-mails you a four-page PDF. One card is made per company every 30 days: a colleague who asks within that time is sent the same card, and each address gets it once.

## What the card measured

- **AI visibility**: the share of ChatGPT's and Gemini's answers that name your brand or your site. These are the engines Ansvisor Starter tracks. Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, which Growth adds, are shown apart in an "In Ansvisor Growth" band. A card made before Ansvisor stopped tracking Microsoft Copilot counts ChatGPT and Copilot instead. Competitors, cited sites and your citations count the same answers.
- The same share for each engine.
- The competitors the answers named most, and the sites they cite, with yours, your competitors' and marketplaces marked.

## The four pages

1. **Status**: the products the card found on your site, the four headline figures, your starting point and first step, each engine, who AI names and cites, and the five questions competitors win most. A visibility of 0% is shown as your starting point.
2. **Questions**: every question, grouped by product, with its keyword's Google searches a month, a grid of what each engine's answer did and the competitor named instead.
3. **Plan**: three findings, each with what you'd do in week one and where Ansvisor helps, the sites AI cites sorted by what you can do there, and how agents work: you approve each step, or, in Growth, chosen agents publish on their own.
4. **Value and plan**: what AI visits are worth, Starter's price, and the free trial.

## Competitors, marketplaces and where to get mentioned

Only brands a buyer could choose instead of you count as competitors. Marketplaces (booking, comparison and listing sites) and other businesses the answers name are listed apart, and the **Named instead** column on the last page names competitors only. A brand written several ways ("Jolly Tur", "Jolly", "jollytur.com") is counted once. When the card suggests a site to get mentioned on, it picks a guide, a publication, an official source or a community that AI cites, never a competitor's site or a marketplace. A review or complaint site such as Şikayetvar or Trustpilot comes only when none of those is cited, with a different step: check and answer what customers say about you there.

## Where the questions come from

The card reads your home page: its title, headings and menus. It names your main products or categories from the words on the page, and writes questions only about those. A question that matches what you sell only loosely is never asked: a card asks fewer questions rather than a wrong one. Questions about your products come first, a few from each, the ones whose keyword gets the most Google searches in your country first; questions about a need your buyers also have fill the rest. Years and seasons in the questions follow the card's date: in November, a holiday question is about the coming winter or next summer, never one already past.

Everything on the card is picked from your results by fixed rules: no model writes its advice.

## Language and currency

The questions and answers are in your target country's language. The PDF and its e-mail are in Turkish for Türkiye and in English elsewhere. Amounts are in US dollars, as Ansvisor's plans are: Google Ads' costs, the value table and the prices. An average order or deal value you gave stays in its own currency.

## Why the app shows other numbers

The card asks each question once, on one day. In Ansvisor, your tracked prompts are asked every day, so the figures move from day to day. The **AI Visibility Score** on **Brand Visibility** is a score from 0 to 100 that also weighs citations and how early you're named, not only the share of answers that name you.

## Measure your own questions every day

The card asks its questions once. To measure them every day, and questions of your own, start the free trial: add your brand in the setup wizard, then add the card's questions and yours as prompts in **Tracked Prompts**, in the same country. Starter tracks 50 prompts a day in ChatGPT and Gemini. **Brand Visibility**, **Competitors** and **Citations** then show the same kind of figures for every day, and the **Action Center** ranks what to do next.

## The value table and the revenue scenario

The last page adds up the Google searches your questions' keywords get a month in your country, and prices 2%, 5% and 10% of them as AI visits at the median Google Ads cost per click of those keywords. It does so only when at least five keywords have a cost per click and the median looks plausible (up to $100 a click). When those clicks cost less than Starter, the card says so instead of claiming a saving. A revenue line appears only if you gave an average order or deal value, and its conversion rate is an assumption. Both are scenarios, not a forecast or a guarantee. **AI Referral Traffic** measures your real visits from AI answers once it's set up.
