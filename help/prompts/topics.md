**Topics** is a tab of the **Prompts** page. It groups your tracked prompts by theme, so you can see how your brand does in AI answers at a higher level than single prompts.

## What each topic shows

Each row shows the topic's name and how many prompts it has, the average visibility across its prompts, its **Trend** against the window of the same length just before, and the **Top competitor** within the topic.

The tab uses the page's date range (24h, 7d, 30d, 90d or custom) and opens on the last 24 hours. It has no Location selector, because topics aren't split by location.

## Open a topic

Click a topic to open its details: how each of its prompts performs, and how often competitors are mentioned within the topic.

## Add a topic

Use **Add Topic** on the tab to add a new topic at any time. You can also add prompts at any time, for example from **Prompt Suggestions**. Tracking picks them up on the next scheduled run.

Keep topic names short (two to four words) and intent-driven: "Pricing" works better than a long descriptive phrase.

## Delete a topic

Admins and managers open the **…** menu at the end of a topic's row and choose **Delete topic**. The topic's prompts aren't deleted: they keep their tracking and results, as prompts without a topic. You can undo the delete for 10 seconds.

## Use topics for planning

- **Editorial planning**: pick the topic with the lowest score and the weakest competitor lead; that's your next content sprint.
- **Reporting**: topic-level stories, such as which themes you own and which you're losing, are easier to share than prompt-level metrics.
- **Spotting drift**: a topic that drops while others stay flat usually signals one competitor's surge or content that has aged.
