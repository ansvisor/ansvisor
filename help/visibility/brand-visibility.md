**Brand Visibility** in the sidebar opens **Answer Engine Insights**, the main analytics page for a brand. It shows how AI engines represent your brand across your tracked prompts, and where to act next.

## What the page shows

- Key metric cards at the top: your visibility score, mentions, citations and tracked prompts, each with its change.
- A trend chart of your visibility, to spot dips and match them to content you published or to competitor activity.
- **By AI platform**: which AI engines do best and worst for your brand. Use it to route content work toward your weakest platform.
- **Topics vs competitors**: your topics against your competitors, shaded light to dark.

## Filters

At the top of the page you can narrow everything to:

- **AI Model**: one AI engine;
- **Region**: one region, when your prompts run in several;
- **Topic**: one topic;
- a date range.

The filters apply to the whole page at once.

## Opportunities

**Opportunities** suggests prompts to track next. **Track** on a suggestion opens **Tracked Prompts** in a new tab, with the new prompts on top.

The volumes shown with these suggestions are an AI's guess, since no keyword behind them is measured yet, so they are always low confidence: grey, from half to twice the guess. Track a prompt to get an estimate from its keywords' Google searches.

## A weekly review

1. Check the brand-level change. If visibility is down by 10% or more, use the trend chart to find when the drop happened.
2. Find the AI platform contributing least: that's your next investment.
3. Look at the suggested prompts and pick one to three to add.
4. Start one piece of content for the prompt that did worst this week.

## When a metric drops sharply

1. Read the AI answers for what dropped. Is your brand still mentioned but ranked lower, or completely absent?
2. Look at the competitors those answers name. A new competitor surge is often the cause.
3. Compare the cited pages with last week's. A retracted article or a new competitor post is often the trigger.
4. Decide what to do: publish content aimed at the prompt, earn a placement on a site AI engines cite, or accept it as a one-time dip.
