**Product Visibility** is the fourth tab of **Visibility**, for brands that sell online. It shows which of your products AI shopping answers put on their shelf, why some don't make it, and what AI visits sell. Open it from the tabs under Visibility's header, or from **Visibility › Products** in the sidebar. It used to be a page of its own in the sidebar; links to it still open it.

## Who sees it

The tab, and **Products** in the sidebar, show for a brand that sells online: one with **Shopping mode** on in its brand settings, or one whose site type is an online store. Your plan needs to include Product Visibility. Other brands see no fourth tab.

When the brand's site is an online store but Shopping mode is off, the tab says so at the top. Admins and managers can click **Turn on Shopping mode** there: it adds ChatGPT Shopping to the brand's prompts. Others see who can turn it on.

## Its views

A second row of tabs holds its views, as on the Mentions tab:

- **Shelf**: your share of the product slots in AI answers, your products on the shelf, AI revenue and the top AI channel; where AI sends shoppers, resellers cheaper than you, and each shopping question's latest shelf, with **Why not you?** where you're missing.
- **Categories**: each category of your catalog, its shelf share and its rival.
- **Products**: your catalog's products with their shelves and readiness, or, before a catalog is connected, the products AI showed.
- **Readiness**: a checklist of what AI shopping needs from your catalog and feed.
- **Competitors**: your competitors' products in AI answers.

## Filters, market and sources

Under the views: **Date range** (7, 30 or 90 days, or All time), **Platform** and **Region**, shared by every view. The **ChatGPT Shopping market** switch picks the US or Europe, where ChatGPT's products come from different sources. The **Data sources** strip shows your product catalog, Google Analytics and Search Console, with **Connect** where one is missing. **Why Ansvisor Product Visibility?** in the header explains what the tab does.

## Loading

The tab opens on the view in its address, the Shelf by default, and loads only that view's figures, all at once. Each other view loads when you open it. A view you opened before shows its figures at once while they're refreshed. A shelf that can't be read says so, with **Try again**, and the rest keeps working.
