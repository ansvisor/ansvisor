ChatGPT Business Hub shows a business a few numbers about itself in ChatGPT, for the last 7 or 28 days only, and has no export. The **ChatGPT Business Hub** card on Visibility's **Overview** keeps those numbers week by week and puts Ansvisor's own ChatGPT numbers for the same days beside them.

## What the card shows

The card appears once your brand tracks ChatGPT and has entered numbers, or has completed the claim step of its Business Hub setup action.

- **Business Hub:** Mentions, Website clicks, Profile views and the top discovery topics, as you entered them. A number Business Hub doesn't show reads **Not shown**.
- **Ansvisor:** your ChatGPT visibility, the answers naming you, the answers citing your site, and the visits that came from ChatGPT, over the same days.

Each number shows its change since the entry before of the same length. Each topic says **Tracked** when you already track a topic of that name, or offers **Suggest prompts**.

## How the numbers differ

Business Hub counts real ChatGPT conversations that referenced your website. Ansvisor counts how often your brand is named, or your site cited, in answers to the prompts you track. Compare their direction, not their size.

Business Hub counts clicks on links in ChatGPT; Ansvisor counts visits that arrived from ChatGPT. Some clicks arrive without a referrer, so the two rarely match.

**How these differ** on the card says the same.

## Enter your numbers

1. In Business Hub, open Insights and pick 7 days.
2. On the card, choose **Enter numbers**.
3. Pick 7 or 28 days and the last day the numbers cover, usually yesterday.
4. Copy Mentions, Website clicks and Profile views, and up to five discovery topics. Leave a number empty if Business Hub doesn't show it.
5. Save.

Entering the same days again replaces that entry. Your first entry completes the setup action's **Enter your first Business Hub numbers** task.

## Who can enter, edit and delete

Every member of the workspace can enter numbers and edit an entry from **All entries**. Only admins and managers can delete an entry.

## Weekly reminder

After your first entry, the Action Center opens **Enter this week's ChatGPT Business Hub numbers** each week your newest entry is 7 days old or more. Business Hub keeps only the last 7 or 28 days, so a week you don't enter is gone from it.

Saving the numbers completes the reminder by itself. Dismissed as **Not now**, it comes back a week later. Dismissed as **Not relevant**, the weekly reminders stop.
