Routine pages waited for a click even when the agent's work was ready.
With an Admin's two switches, an agent in Full auto sends or publishes up to the daily limit you set.
A page still waits for you when it fails Quality, your brand facts or the other safety checks.
Turn on Agents that publish on their own in Settings › Chat, then allow it on the agent.
