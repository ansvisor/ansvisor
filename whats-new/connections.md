Connected tools were spread across settings, so it was hard to see what a brand had.
Connections lists each tool with its status, account and the skills and agents that use it.
You see what a tool would unlock before you connect it, and what would stop if you disconnect it.
Open Connections in the Chat menu; an Admin connects a tool, and others ask their Admin.
