Repeating the same preferences in every chat took time.
Start a message with "Remember that…" and Chat saves one short line once you click Save.
Notes for you, a brand or the workspace shape every chat, and the steps show when one was used.
Change or delete notes on the Memory page, from the ⋯ menu at the top of a chat.
