Asking Chat about one sentence of a page meant copying it into the chat by hand.
Select text in the Content Editor and click Ask Chat or press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K to put it in the Chat tab as a quote.
The quote waits in the Chat box and nothing is sent until you send it, so you can say what to change.
Select a passage in the page, click Ask Chat, then write your question in the Chat tab and send it.
