It was hard to approve a publish or send when you could only see a short preview of the draft.
Publish, CMS and webhook approval cards have a Show full text button that opens the whole draft.
You read the draft as it will go out, and it can't be changed there.
Click Show full text on the card, read the draft, then approve as usual.
