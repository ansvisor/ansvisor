Some audit checks matter more for AI citations than others, and the report didn't say which.
Each check now shows Strong evidence, Likely helps or Hygiene, and llms.txt no longer counts toward the score.
You fix what has the best evidence first, and a page's next audit may move a point or two on its own.
Open a report and click Formula to see the weights and the levels.
