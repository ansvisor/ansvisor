Each brand has words it never uses and sites it doesn't want to cite.
Content rules lists banned phrases, blocked and allowed domains, and can limit sources to the allowed ones.
Chat and agents rewrite a draft with a banned phrase instead of saving it, and never suggest a blocked domain.
Admins and Managers can open the brand's settings, go to General and fill in Content rules.
