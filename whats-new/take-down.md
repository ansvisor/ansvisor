Pulling a page that went live used to mean finding it in your CMS.
Take down removes a page from your Webflow, WordPress or Wix site and keeps it there as a draft.
A page you took down never goes live on its own again, even from an agent.
Admins and Managers pick Take down in the page's … menu in the Library, or on an agent's notice.
