It was easy to fix a page and never learn whether the fix worked.
After a change, Chat offers a check in 7, 14, 30 or 90 days that reads your tracking data.
You get Improved, No meaningful change or Declined in the chat and at the top of your Inbox.
Click Set check on the card Chat shows after a change you made or approved.
