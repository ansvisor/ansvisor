Weekly reports and checks meant asking Chat the same thing every week.
Say "every Monday at 9:00" and Chat shows a card you can edit; nothing is scheduled until you click.
Each run lands in your Inbox or your email, and a repeat can say No change when nothing moved.
Admins and Managers ask in any chat, then manage their jobs on the Scheduled page in the Chat menu.
